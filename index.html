    About MY QS

    A professional quantity surveying and construction cost consultancy built on precision, transparency and commercial intelligence.

    Our Story

    Measured advice.
    Commercial clarity.

    MY QS was founded to give Kenyan developers, contractors and institutions access to the kind of disciplined, independent quantity surveying that international projects rely on — delivered locally, with deep understanding of how construction is actually procured and managed in Kenya.

    We work alongside project teams from the earliest feasibility conversations through tender, construction and final account. Every figure we produce is traceable, and every recommendation is made in our client's commercial interest — never anyone else's.

    Our clients include private developers, family offices, institutions and contractors who value accurate numbers and honest advice.
